I ask because those two words get used interchangeably but they're testing completely different things. Feasibility is a technical and resource claim: can we build this with what we have, time, skill, infrastructure. Desirability is a human claim: do people want this, does it fit how they actually behave. If your teammate says "not feasible" but what they mean is "I don't think users will adopt it" or "I personally wouldn't use it," that's not a technical judgment at all, it's a taste judgment wearing a technical costume.
